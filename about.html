About us

Axis Port LTD

A United Kingdom trading company built on sourcing discipline, logistics that hold up under pressure, and documentation you can audit.

Who we are

Trade with a fixed point of reference

Axis Port LTD is registered in England & Wales (company number 17395908) at 128 City Road, London EC1V 2NX.

We specialise in agricultural goods, household items and electronics, and petrol chemicals. Around each of those lines we operate an integrated service — strategic sourcing, quality control, chartering and export documentation — so a client deals with one accountable party rather than a chain of intermediaries.

We source from producers in Canada, the United Kingdom, Eastern Europe and Central Asia, and supply processors and end users across the United Kingdom, Europe, Africa, the Middle East and Asia. Contracting with a London company gives our counterparties a familiar legal and banking framework.

We do not source from, sell to or ship through sanctioned jurisdictions. Every counterparty is screened against United Kingdom, European Union and United States sanctions lists before we trade.

How we work

One team, from purchase order to discharge

Sourcing, inspection and shipping are handled by the same team, which means a problem at load port does not become a problem the buyer discovers at discharge. Cargo is supervised by recognised survey companies, and export documentation is prepared in full before shipment.

With a forward approach and a commitment to excellence, we serve global markets with full transparency, accountability and quality assurance.
