What we trade

Three commodity lines, each handled end to end — sourcing, inspection, chartering and documentation.

01 — Agricultural Goods

Grains, pulses and oilseeds

As specialists in agricultural exports, we facilitate the global trade and distribution of high-quality grains, pulses, oilseeds and their processing products. We are focused on long-term cooperation and operate with an absolute commitment to quality, with cargo supervision by leading survey companies on every shipment.

  • Grains & cereals: corn, barley, wheat and oats.
  • Pulses & legumes: chickpeas, green and yellow peas, lentils.
  • Oilseeds: sunflower, flax, safflower and mustard seed.
  • Processing products: meals, oils and by-products.

Full export documentation, quality certificates and professional cargo surveys are prepared as standard.

02 — Household Items & Electronics

Consumer goods that arrive as specified

We supply a broad range of household items and electronic products that combine quality, reliability and affordability. Leveraging our established logistics and trade expertise, we handle sourcing, consolidation and delivery under the same standards that govern our commodity business.

  • Electronics: consumer devices, components and accessories.
  • Household goods: kitchenware, small appliances and everyday essentials.
  • Supply: container-load and mixed-consignment shipments.
  • Compliance: product conformity and labelling handled before dispatch.

Pre-shipment inspection is available on request for buyers who require it.

03 — Petrol Chemicals

Chemical cargo, handled with discipline

We trade petrol chemical products, working with refiners, producers and industrial end users. Chemical cargo leaves no room for improvisation: every transaction runs on verified quantity and specification, independent inspection and a documentary chain that stands up to scrutiny.

  • Polymers: polyethylene, polypropylene and polyvinyl chloride, bagged or in containers.
  • Aromatics & olefins: benzene, toluene, xylene, ethylene and propylene.
  • Solvents & base chemicals: methanol, glycols and industrial alcohols.
  • Inspection: independent quantity and specification survey at load and discharge.
  • Terms: Free On Board and Cost, Insurance and Freight structures, with recognised documentary instruments.

All trade is conducted in accordance with United Kingdom and international sanctions, chemical-compliance and dangerous-goods requirements. We do not source petrol chemicals from any sanctioned jurisdiction.
